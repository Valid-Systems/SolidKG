# Tested codebases

SolidKG's extraction, resolution, and retrieval work is evaluated against public projects in its benchmark and playbook suites, with a broad reference corpus extending source coverage. This inventory identifies those repositories and makes every entry directly inspectable.

Benchmark and playbook entries have recorded evaluation evidence. The July 2026 reference snapshot contained 112 public repositories; its entries record corpus inclusion, not a separate performance claim for every repository.
